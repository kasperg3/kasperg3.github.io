Now

Two retrieval problems under one roof: a stock library, where dense embeddings do the work, and a customer's own archive, where late interaction over the page keeps the evidence a single vector throws away.

The models run on our own hardware, which is a sovereignty decision before it is a cost one. The Colourbox lecture is the long version; the search on this site is the small one you can poke at.
